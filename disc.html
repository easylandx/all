The rules we build by

A few working principles keep us from turning your page into a junk drawer.

RULE 01

The 90% rule

Every feature request gets scored: does it clearly help this page hit its one goal? Anything short of a definite yes is treated as a no. "Might be nice" doesn't make the cut.

RULE 02

Don't patch a sinking site

If your current website has been rebuilt, re-plugged, and re-themed for three years running, the question isn't what to add next — it's whether you'd choose that site again, starting from zero.

RULE 03

Protect the first impression

The page a stranger sees first is not a place to save money on slow-loading, cluttered guesswork. It's the asset your whole marketing budget depends on — worth building right, once.
