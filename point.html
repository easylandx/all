Lesson 1 · Overstories

The headline is the canopy.

In forestry, the overstory is the tall canopy that decides how much sun reaches the floor. On a landing page, your top message decides what every visitor does below it. Don't police individual clicks. Rewrite the overstory. Pick one and watch the floor.

Lesson 2 · The Magic Third

Give one message 30%+ of the page.

Gladwell shows that a minority between roughly 25% and 33% flips a group's dynamics. A message that's 10% of your page is noise. At critical mass it changes the culture of the whole page. Drag to give your single offer more room.

Lesson 3 · Superspreaders

Three elements carry the epidemic.

A tiny fraction of actors drives most of any contagion. On a landing page they're the headline, the main button and the proof. Click a small node, then click a hub. Notice where the reach comes from.

Lesson 5 · The Ethics of Social Engineering

Engineer. Never exploit.

The same mechanisms that spread good ideas can manipulate people. EasyLandX uses them only for the visitor's benefit.
