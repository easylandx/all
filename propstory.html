Four truths

What a landing page actually is

A useful shift in thinking: your page isn't reporting on some private brand essence. It's the thing that creates the impression in the first place — and that's good news, because it means every part of it is yours to shape.

01 — No inner world required

Trust lives on the page, not behind it

Visitors never see your mission statement in your head. They see words, colors, and proof on a screen — and build every impression from exactly that.

02 — A useful shorthand

"This brand feels trustworthy" is a helpful story

Calling a business "reliable" or "premium" isn't a literal fact about its soul — it's a shorthand visitors use to predict how you'll treat them. That shorthand is worth designing for.

03 — Props, not magic

Every element on the page is a prop in that story

A testimonial, a founder photo, a clear price — each one hands visitors something concrete to build their impression from.

04 — Keep the story, skip the mystery

You don't need to abandon "brand feel" — just locate it correctly

Brand personality stays a genuinely useful idea. It simply lives in your visible choices, ready to be shaped on purpose.

The boot analogy

"Literally false, practically useful" — and that's the point

Saying "Rome sits in the shin of the boot" isn't a literal fact — Italy isn't footwear. Yet it hands anyone looking at a map exactly the information they need. Your landing page copy works the same way.

Calling your service "the easy button for small business websites" isn't literally true either. It's a story that accurately hands visitors what they need to understand you fast — and that's precisely why it earns its place on the page.

Essential lessons

Three ideas worth building into every page

01

The story is metaphorical, not literal

"This brand feels premium" is a projection built from real, external choices — a photo, a font, a price — not a hidden fact you're reporting on.

02

Meaning starts on the page, not in your head

The clearest way to shape how visitors see your business is to shape exactly what they can see — words, proof, and layout come first.

03

Trust lives across every touchpoint

A landing page, a WhatsApp reply, and a follow-up email all add props to the same story. Consistency across each one keeps that story coherent.
