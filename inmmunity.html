The core problem

You keep treating an adaptive problem with a technical fix.

Kegan and Lahey's research on change applies almost exactly to why teams stall on landing pages: the obstacle isn't the builder, the template, or the copy deck. It's a mindset shift you haven't made yet — and no new tool substitutes for that.

Technical challenge

What you keep buying

  • A prettier template or a new page builder
  • Another A/B testing plugin
  • A faster hosting stack
  • More stock photography, more icons, more sections
Adaptive challenge

What actually needs to change

  • Trusting a page at 80% instead of hunting for 100%
  • Letting real visitors judge the page instead of you
  • Publishing before every stakeholder signs off
  • Treating "live" as a starting point, not a verdict
