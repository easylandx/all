The six minds, applied

One page, six cognitive systems to design for

A visitor experiences your landing page as a single moment. Underneath, six separate mental systems are doing the work. easylandx optimizes for all six at once.

1
Vision & Attention
Where are they looking?

Visual processing runs largely on autopilot, filtering out anything that doesn't fit a clear pattern. easylandx layouts use deliberate hierarchy and clean spacing so the eye lands exactly where it should, without conscious effort.

Automatic visual hierarchy across every template
2
Wayfinding
Where do they go next?

Visitors navigate a page the way they'd navigate an airport terminal — always asking where they are, where they can go, and how to get back. easylandx keeps a sticky nav and clear section markers in view, so orientation never breaks.

Built-in sticky navigation and section anchors
3
Memory
What do they expect?

Every visitor arrives with a mental model built from every other page they've used. easylandx templates follow familiar conventions — cart icons, form patterns, button placement — so your page feels instantly recognizable.

Convention-matched components, out of the box
4
Language
What words do they use?

The words on your page need to match the words already in your visitor's head. easylandx's copy tools flag corporate jargon and suggest phrasing closer to how your actual audience talks.

Audience-matched microcopy suggestions
5
Decision-Making
How do they choose?

Visitors rarely compare every option in detail — they pick the first choice that clearly meets their need. easylandx keeps plan and offer selections short and simple, with one obvious path to the next step.

Simplified pricing and offer layouts
6
Emotion
How do they feel?

Emotion shapes every step of how a visitor processes your page, from first glance to final click. easylandx forms use reassuring progress cues and clear, friendly messaging to keep the experience calm and confident.

Reassuring progress indicators and friendly form copy
