Five lessons, five landing page moves

What changes when everyone has the same AI tools, and what your page should do about it.

Arbitrage is dead

Platform tricks are automated by Performance Max and Advantage+. Execution is equal for all, so strategy is the moat. Your page wins on positioning, not hacks.

The funnel is a mess

Buyers loop through a non-linear messy middle of recall and category triggers. Build one page that welcomes a first-timer and a returning buyer alike.

Be mentally available

Growth comes from light and occasional buyers, not only super-fans. Use distinct brand assets (color, line, shape) so people remember you at the trigger moment.

Copilot, not oracle

AI spots patterns and drafts fast, but lacks empathy, taste and foresight. Let it draft variants; you pick the one that sounds like your customer.

Escape the attribution trap

Last-click flatters the final touch. Judge the page by profit, incrementality and lifetime value, not just clicks.
