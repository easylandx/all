Why small businesses never launch a page

Five walls, and how we take each one down

Ziglar taught that every objection traces back to one of five root causes. The same five show up whenever a small business owner puts off getting a real website.

1

No Need — "My customers just call me anyway"

We show you what those customers see instead: nothing, or a rival's page, the moment they search your name.

2

No Money — "A website feels expensive"

We price a single focused page at small-business rates, and frame it against the cost of the customers currently walking past you.

3

No Desire — "It's not urgent this month"

We keep the ask small and concrete — one page, one clear job — so it never competes with the rest of your to-do list.

4

No Hurry — "I'll get to it eventually"

We show a working draft fast, built from what you already have, so "eventually" becomes something you can react to today.

5

No Trust — "How do I know it'll be good?"

You see your actual finished page, built from your real business, before deciding on anything further.
