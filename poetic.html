Part One — The Excavation

Find the message that makes people act

The strongest pages do not hide behind vague promises. They make the value obvious, specific, and compelling — because clarity is what makes people stop, understand, and act.

The Word

"Generic copy gets ignored. Specific copy gives people a reason to care."

Replace vague promises with the details that make your offer credible: the real benefit, the real customer, the real outcome, the reason someone should choose you.
