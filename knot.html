Three ways people get entangled

Wanting two incompatible things at once is what keeps a launch stuck. Recognise the type, then loosen it.

Time-based

Trapped by sunk costs or by polishing before anything exists.

False constraint: "I have already spent too long to launch something small."

Social

Bound by a phantom audience, or by someone else's scorecard.

False constraint: "Everyone will compare my page to bigger brands."

Identity-based

Holding on to who you used to be, or to promises made to a past self.

False constraint: "I swore I would only launch with a full brand."

Do not force it. Change the conditions.

Persistent issues are rarely personal failure. They are usually held in place by a system that quietly rewards the status quo, such as long approval chains, endless revisions, and unclear goals. Pushing harder only tightens the knot.
