Breadth over depth

A single rigid plan leans on many assumptions. A wide set of options keeps your page resilient when audiences, seasons and markets shift.

Key lessons

Four lessons, built into every page

📐

Life is not a straight line

One path creates blind spots. Pages with several entry points welcome visitors who arrive from every direction.

🎲

Embrace candidate moves

Generate a wide set of options, even unusual ones, before committing to your headline and offer.

⏱️

Focus under pressure

Spend limited attention on the moments that change the outcome: the first screen, the offer and the button.

🔄

Learn from blunders

Spot a weak position early, keep the lesson, and pivot sideways instead of doubling down.
