Why most sites never go live

It's rarely the budget. It's the freeze.

Sales research on 2.5 million B2B conversations found the majority of stalled deals don't lose to a competitor — they lose to the buyer's own indecision. The same three friction points show up whenever a small business owner tries to get a website built.

Friction 01

Too many ways to build it

Wix, Squarespace, a cheap template, a cousin who "knows HTML," a freelancer on a forum — every option looks half-right, so nothing gets picked. Endless choice becomes no choice.

Friction 02

Endless research, no launch

Tutorials, reviews, comparison threads, "best website builder 2026" articles — the research never really ends, and every new article resets the decision back to zero.

Friction 03

Fear it won't bring customers

Even once a design is chosen, the real fear surfaces: what if it looks unprofessional, loads slowly, or simply doesn't convert visitors into paying customers?
