Four rules we build by

Praise publicly, correct privately

Show real client wins up front. Handle objections gently in the FAQ, never by scolding the reader.

Ask, do not order

Questions like "What would your first booking look like?" pull people in. Commands push them away.

Control your reaction

Calm, steady design anchors anxious visitors. No clutter, no panic timers.

Invest in people

Your page exists to grow your visitor's confidence, not to inflate your ego.
