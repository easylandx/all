Three big ideas

What chess teaches about great pages

Diego Rasskin-Gutman's Chess Metaphors uses 64 squares and 32 pieces as a cognitive laboratory. Three of its messages shape how easylandx builds.

1. Chess is a mirror of the mind

Memory, pattern recognition, emotion, calculation and creativity all meet on the board. A landing page brings the same abilities together, so easylandx designs for the whole visitor.

2. A game is a dialogue

Two minds take turns sending and receiving messages through pieces and squares. Your page speaks first, your visitor replies with a scroll, a tap or a click, and the exchange flows.

3. Human insight shines

Machines bring speed, people bring intuition, structure and creative leaps. easylandx pages celebrate the human side: clear shape, meaningful patterns, a memorable idea.
