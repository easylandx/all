The easylandx method

A five‑step path from blank page to breakthrough page

Adapted from connective‑thinking research: great pages come from linking your goal to ideas that, at first glance, have nothing to do with it.

STEP 01

Deconstruct your goal

Name the one action you want a visitor to take, and the single feeling the page should leave them with.

STEP 02

Bring in fresh stimuli

Pull in a layout, industry, or object that has nothing to do with your page. Let it sit next to your goal.

STEP 03

Force the connection

Ask how that unrelated idea could shape your hero, your copy, or your call to action. Write every option down.

STEP 04

Map the pattern

Find the underlying structure that made the unrelated idea work, and translate it into blocks on your page.

STEP 05

Converge and launch

Choose your strongest combination, refine the details, and publish straight to easylandx.

Why pairing unrelated ideas works

Original page ideas rarely start from a blank template — they start at the meeting point of two things that were never in the same room before. The Spark Generator gives you that meeting point instantly.

  • Every spark pairs a real kind of business with a trait borrowed from an everyday object.
  • Use the pairing to reshape your hero section, your imagery, or the rhythm of your copy.
  • Generate a few sparks in a row and keep the one with the most energy — quantity opens the door to quality.
