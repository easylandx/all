The questioning sequence

Four kinds of question, in order, every time.

Before any page is designed, a conversation runs through the same four stages — the order matters more than any single question in it.

S

Situation

A few facts about how the business runs today — kept brief, because most of this should already be known before asking.

"What are you using for your site right now?"
P

Problem

Where the current setup is quietly costing time, leads, or credibility — the dissatisfaction the owner feels but hasn't priced.

"How long does a new page actually take to go live?"
I

Implication

What that delay or that generic page actually costs downstream — in ad spend, in launches missed, in customers who leave.

"What happens to that campaign while the page is still in progress?"
N

Need-payoff

The owner states the value themselves — the most convincing case for a custom page is always the one they make out loud.

"What would it be worth to have that live by Friday?"
