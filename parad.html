The Problem

Why unlimited options quietly wreck your launch

Regret

The template you didn't pick

Faced with 400 templates, it's easy to imagine the one you skipped would have converted better. That doubt shows up before you've even published.

Opportunity Cost

Every unused feature nags at you

Choosing a layout means giving up every other layout's strengths. A drag-and-drop builder with infinite blocks turns "done" into a moving target.

Escalation

Pixel-perfect or nothing

When a tool can theoretically do anything, "good" no longer feels good enough. You keep tweaking spacing instead of shipping the page.

Self-Blame

Low conversions feel personal

With one simple option, a slow launch is the tool's fault. With a thousand settings, a weak page feels like a failure of your own judgment.
