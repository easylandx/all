What 10% Happier teaches a landing page

Dan Harris found a practical, science-backed path to calm focus and shared it with high achievers who want to keep their drive. EasyLandX turns each of his lessons into a way of building pages that welcome visitors with clarity.

1

From the book: The voice in the head

Answer the inner voice

Every visitor arrives with a running commentary of questions. A great page hears each one and answers it, in the order it appears. Try it in the next section.

2

From the book: A new relationship with thoughts

Guide attention with clarity

Mindfulness is about changing how we relate to thoughts, so every visitor feels at ease and in charge. EasyLandX pages guide attention one idea at a time, and plenty of content fits when each part has a purpose.

3

From the book: Meditation made practical

Build on practical, proven structure

Mindfulness works like strength training for focus. EasyLandX pages work like a training plan: headline, benefits, proof, offer, and one button, in the order visitors recognize.

4

From the book: 10% is a game-changer

Compound small improvements

A 10% clearer headline, a 10% friendlier button, a 10% faster page. Small gains multiply over months, and the Compounder below shows how far seven steps can go.

5

From the book: You keep your edge

Be calm and driven

Responding with clarity sharpens focus. Pages that feel calm convert with confidence, and your ambition stays in the lead.
