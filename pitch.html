The big idea

Buying is harder than selling.

Visitors meet crowded choices, big decisions, and plenty of questions. A landing page succeeds when it behaves like a trusted guide: it brings clarity, lowers the effort of deciding, and makes the next step feel natural.

So many options, so many tabs. This is what a visitor feels before they find a guide.

Be the trusted guide

The goal of a page is to help visitors choose with confidence, not to push. Calm, clear storytelling does that best.

Positioning drives the page

Positioning on paper comes alive when it shapes the live narrative. Your landing page is positioning in action.

Go beyond the feature list

A menu of features is easy to skim past. A cohesive solution to a specific problem is easy to remember.

Lead with differentiated value

Features are easy to copy. The value only you deliver, compared with real alternatives, is what turns interest into action.

Put it to work

Three habits that make the story shine.

HABIT 1

Discover during the alternatives

Instead of a rigid checklist, ask how people handle trade-offs today. Their answers shape the page, the form, and the follow-up conversation.

HABIT 2

Pilot, learn, refine

Try the new story with a small group first. Real conversations show which lines resonate, then the best version goes everywhere.

HABIT 3

Tell one story everywhere

Use the same narrative across your landing page, social posts, decks, videos, and messages so every touchpoint feels like one brand.
