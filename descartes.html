Why feeling comes first

Long before a visitor reads a headline, their body has already reacted. Color, shape, rhythm, and pacing trigger a quick physical response — call it a gut sense — that shapes everything that follows. A page that earns a warm, easy first reaction gives the reasoning mind a welcoming place to land.

"Feeling opens the page. Reasoning keeps the visitor there."

Once that initial pull is in place, the visitor's conscious mind steps in to confirm it: clear value, honest proof, and a confident next step. Pages that carry both layers, felt and reasoned, move people to act with real conviction rather than hesitation.
