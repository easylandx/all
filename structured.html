The big ideas of the book

Three messages that change your page

Arthur Benjamin, mathematician and showman, proves that analytical thinking gives a decisive edge. Here is how each core message becomes a landing page advantage.

1

Luck can be managed & quantified

Games of chance are not pure mystery. By weighing probabilities and expected values you shift the odds your way over time. A landing page works the same way: visitors, conversion rate and value per customer tell you exactly where your edge lives.

2

Systematic thinking beats intuition

Sudoku, Wordle and the Rubik's Cube open up when you use algorithms, symmetry and information theory. Your page gets the same treatment: a repeatable structure for headline, proof, offer and call to action that you refine step by step.

3

Math is a real-life decision tool

Risk management, optimal wagering and un-exploitable play reach well beyond the table. They guide business choices: how much to invest in a campaign, which offer to test and how to design a page that wins across many kinds of visitors.

Core lessons & frameworks

Four frameworks. One winning page.

Each lesson from the book, followed by the exact way EasyLandX applies it to landing pages.

Lesson 1Probability & Expected Value

Book: Judge every move by its long-term average outcome. A decision earns its place when the mathematical expectation is positive, not when one lucky result appears.

On your landing page Treat each visitor as one roll of the dice. EV per visitor = conversion rate × value per customer. Track the average, celebrate each lift in it, and invest where the number grows. Use the EV Calculator below to see your own edge.

Lesson 2Game Theory & Un-exploitable Play

Book: Against active opponents, mix your strategy so no pattern can be exploited. Nash equilibria and Rock-Paper-Scissors show that unpredictability is a strength.

On your landing page Your market is a living contest: competitors adapt, audiences vary. Keep several headline and offer variants in rotation, test them side by side, and let the data guide the mix. A page that speaks to different visitor types is a page rivals cannot copy with a single move.

Lesson 3Information Reduction & Elimination

Book: The best opening move is the one that teaches you the most. Guesses like CRANE or SLATE shrink the Wordle word list fast by revealing common letters.

On your landing page Make your first version a learning move. Launch a page with one clear promise, one primary button and a short form, then read what visitors do. Each click, scroll and reply removes possibilities, so your second version starts far ahead of a guess.

Lesson 4Working Backward & Invariants

Book: Begin with the endgame. In Nim and other combinatorial games, positions are either winning for the player to move (N-positions) or strong for the player who just moved (P-positions). Guide the game toward the second kind.

On your landing page Define the final position first: a booked call, a purchase, a sign-up. Then place each section as a step that leads to it, and keep every step in a position of strength: clear message, visible proof, one easy next move. Try it in the Nim game below.

🪙 Nim: Work Backward & Win

Take any number of stones from one row by clicking a stone (it and every stone to its right are taken). Whoever takes the last stone wins. The secret: keep the XOR of the row sizes at zero after your move. That is a P-position, the winning spot for the player who just moved.

Key strategies for specific games

The playbook: from the table to your page

Every game in the book teaches a move you can reuse. Here is the translation into landing page strategy.

Blackjack
The bookFollow basic strategy to shrink the house edge to its minimum, and track high versus low cards to bet boldly when the deck favors you.
Your landing pageStart from proven patterns: clear hero, social proof, single call to action. Watch your running numbers, and invest more when traffic quality favors you.
Wagering
The bookUse the Kelly Criterion and Gambler's Ruin formula for exact bet sizes that protect the bankroll and grow it steadily.
Your landing pageSize each ad budget to your measured edge. Scale up gradually as results confirm the numbers, and keep a reserve for the next experiment.
Wordle
The bookOpen with high-entropy guesses full of common vowels and consonants to narrow the list quickly.
Your landing pageOpen with the most informative test: headline and main offer. Each answer from visitors trims the list of options for the next version.
Poker
The bookCompare pot odds with the chance of improving your hand, and mix your strategy so your strength stays hidden.
Your landing pageCompare cost per visitor with the value they bring. Proceed when the odds favor you, and vary your messages across audiences.
Backgammon
The bookBlend tactical probability, such as hit chances per roll, with strategic doubling-cube choices based on win probabilities.
Your landing pagePair daily tactics (button color, form length) with strategic bets (new offer, new audience). Double down when the data shows momentum.
Nim · Sudoku · Cube
The bookUse symmetry, solve sub-problems step by step with algorithms, and reduce the search space through logical deduction.
Your landing pageBuild in balanced, repeatable sections. Improve one element at a time, lock in each win, and let logic guide the next change.
