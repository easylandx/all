Why noise sinks a landing page

Most pages don't lose visitors on quality. They lose them to noise.

A visitor scanning your page is doing the same thing a decision-maker does under pressure: filtering a flood of input for the one signal that actually matters. Autoplay banners, three competing buttons, and paragraphs of unnecessary copy all count as noise — and noise is what sends a visitor elsewhere. Every EasyLandX page is built with a deliberate process for finding the signal and cutting everything around it.

Signal vs. noise

A simple filter for every element on your page.

Before a page goes live, we sort everything on it into two columns. Only one of them makes the final cut.

Noise
  • Several buttons, each asking for something different
  • Stock imagery that says nothing about your business
  • Long paragraphs before the actual offer appears
  • Autoplay video or motion that pulls focus away from the message
  • Jargon that describes the business, not the customer's outcome
Signal
  • One clear action, stated in plain language
  • A headline that says exactly what you offer and to whom
  • Real proof — a result, a review, a number that matters
  • Enough white space for the message to stand on its own
  • Words your customer would use to describe their own need
