01 · The Core Philosophy

Stop competing on price. Compete in a category of one.

Racing to the bottom on price destroys margin and invites commodity comparison. The goal is a high-ticket offer so specific and so valuable that competitors become irrelevant to the conversation.

The Value Equation
Value =
Dream Outcome
Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
↑ maximize both
Time Delay
Perceived Effort & Sacrifice
↓ minimize both
02 · Identifying Your Dream Market

Pick the market before you pick the offer.

Criterion

Massive pain

The problem is urgent enough that people are already actively looking for a way out.

Criterion

Purchasing power

The audience has the means to pay a premium for a real solution, not just interest in one.

Criterion

Easy to target

You can find and reach this group through identifiable channels, communities, or platforms.

Criterion

Growing market

The category is expanding, not shrinking — tailwind instead of headwind.

Narrowing to a specific niche lets you customize the offer, charge meaningfully more, and speak directly to one audience's exact pain — instead of a diluted message for everyone.

03 · Step-by-Step Offer Creation

Four steps, in order, no skipping.

01

Identify problems

List every obstacle a customer faces before, during, and after trying to reach their goal — not just the obvious one.

02

Generate solutions

Convert each problem into a clear, corresponding solution feature. Every listed pain point earns a matching fix.

03

Determine delivery vehicles

Decide how each solution actually gets delivered — coaching, a course, done-for-you service, or software.

04

Trim and stack

Cut anything low-value and high-effort to fulfill. Stack the highest-value pieces into one irresistible package.

04 · Enhancing the Offer

The offer is built. Now make it undeniable.

S

Scarcity & urgency

Limit available seats or set firm deadlines that force a real decision.

G

Unbeatable guarantees

Remove risk with conditional, unconditional, or anti-guarantees — pay them back if it doesn't work.

B

Strategic bonuses

Frame components as free, high-value bonuses instead of folding everything into one bundle.

N

Naming secrets

Use a formula to build a name people repeat back to you unprompted.
