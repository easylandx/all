The EasyLandX discipline framework

Five steps that lower the cost of finishing a page, built on how the prefrontal cortex actually works. Tap each one to open it.

Progress: tap a step to open it

Optimize your building window
Protect the hours when your focus is highest.
Sleep, food, and stress all shape how much prefrontal capacity you have on a given day. EasyLandX lets you save a draft mid-thought, so you can match the hardest sections to your sharpest hours instead of forcing them at the wrong time.
2
Remove the temptations, not just resist them
Design the builder so distraction has nowhere to sit.
A focused, single-purpose canvas means there's no scattered settings menu competing for attention. The environment does the resisting, so your discipline is free for the words and design choices only you can make.
3
Apply the 10-second rule
Delay the urge to switch templates.
When the pull to restart or redesign hits, EasyLandX prompts a short pause first. Most of that pull fades before the pause ends — try the live timer below.
4
Pre-script your If-Then moves
Decide your response before the moment arrives.
Implementation intentions remove the need to decide in real time. Build your own with the tool below, then keep it visible while you work.
5
Reframe the friction
Read resistance as a sign of real progress.
The moment a section feels hardest is usually the moment closest to being done. EasyLandX marks near-finished sections clearly, so the friction reads as proof of progress instead of a stopping point.
