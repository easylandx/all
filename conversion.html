Six Laws We Design Around

Every page has two faces: the words on it, and the psychology underneath.

The Underlying Law
Law 01 — Irrationality

Emotion arrives first, logic follows.

Most pages lead with a feature list, hoping logic will win the visitor over.

Design principle

A visitor decides how they feel about your page before they've read a single line. EasyLandX opens every design with the feeling your business should evoke, then lets the details back it up.

Law 02 — Narcissism

Everyone is the hero of their own story.

Many pages open with "We are the best at..." and talk about the business first.

Design principle

A visitor is scanning for themselves, not for you. Your copy should mirror their goal back to them in the first line, so they see their own reflection before yours.

Law 03 — Role-Playing

What people say they want isn't always what they do.

A contact form and a launch date are treated as the finish line.

Design principle

Words are a mask; behavior is the tell. Where a visitor pauses, scrolls back, or hovers tells the real story. EasyLandX pages are built to be read this way, then refined against what visitors actually do.

Law 04 — Compulsivity

Character shows up under pressure, not in a pitch.

A polished promise of "amazing results" is placed front and center.

Design principle

A visitor trusts a track record more than a slogan. Real results, reviews, and before-and-afters carry more weight than any adjective — so that's where the page puts its emphasis.

Law 05 — Shortsightedness

Trends fade. Trust compounds.

This year's trending layout is copied because it's what's popular right now.

Design principle

A page built purely for this quarter's trend ages out fast. EasyLandX designs for the next few years of your brand, so the page still feels like you long after the trend has moved on.

Law 06 — Defensiveness

Pressure raises guards. Warmth lowers them.

A countdown timer and a pop-up push the visitor toward an urgent decision.

Design principle

A visitor who feels cornered leaves. One who feels understood, stays. EasyLandX pages earn the decision with clarity and warmth, so saying yes feels like the visitor's own idea.

The Shadow Traits We Design Against

Greene's darker chapters are a warning label for landing pages, too.

Every one of these instincts can leak into a page. Naming them is how EasyLandX keeps them out of yours.

Envy

Credit shared, not hoarded

Real client wins and reviews are shown as they are — specific and honest — rather than inflated into a boast that invites doubt.

Grandiosity

Claims grounded in fact

A page that promises everything trusts nothing to its own strengths. Every claim on an EasyLandX page is one your business can actually stand behind.

Aggression

Persuasion without pressure

Urgency has its place, but a page that leans on it constantly reads as aggression. Warm, clear, confident — that's the register EasyLandX writes in.
