Core lessons

Four foundations of an owned page

Each lesson from the book becomes a daily habit for page builders. Together they turn a landing page into a mission with a clear leader.

Absolute Accountability

Leaders own everything in their world. When results arrive, the first step is a look in the mirror, and the next step is a confident improvement.

On your page

The headline, images, form and speed are all yours to shape. Review the page, choose one refinement, relaunch and celebrate the progress.

Humility Fuels Growth

Humility opens the door to feedback, learning and stronger collaboration. A humble leader grows faster and brings the team along.

On your page

Let visitor data crown the winning headline, even when it is the version a teammate wrote. Every test result is a gift that sharpens the page.

Total Alignment

Every team member understands what they are doing and why they are doing it. The book calls this the commander's intent, and it powers confident action.

On your page

Write one sentence that explains the purpose of the page, then share it with writers, designers and developers. The tool below makes it easy.

Simplicity Drives Execution

Clear, concise plans stay powerful when circumstances shift. Simple instructions let every team member act with speed and confidence.

On your page

One promise, one primary button and three benefits. Visitors understand the page within seconds and know exactly what to do next.
