Part Two — Mindset

The Traps Keeping Your Page Offline

Most unpublished pages aren't a design problem. They're a mindset problem wearing a design costume.

01

Procrastination Is Not Preparation

"Later" isn't a peaceful pause — it's a trap. Endless revisions and waiting for "the right time to launch" are usually fear of judgment wearing the mask of careful planning.

02

Action Beats Planning

A flawless mockup that never leaves Figma is worth less than a live page with one clumsy sentence. Momentum comes exclusively from shipping something — not from perfecting a plan that stays on the page.

03

Your Tools Are Your Environment

Slow builders, bloated templates, and agencies that take months quietly erode your urgency. Ruthlessly avoid the tools that make "later" the default.
