The SB7 framework

Seven beats. One story your customer already wants to be in.

Every landing page easylandx generates follows the same arc that's driven storytelling for millennia — reworked as a path from confusion to a "buy now" click.

THE HERO'S ARC — FROM DESIRE, THROUGH CONFLICT, TO A GUIDED SUCCESS (DASHED PATH = THE FAILURE THEY AVOID)

Beat 01 — A Character

Your customer

Has a clear, specific desire related to what you sell. Everything on the page starts from that desire, not from your product.

Beat 02 — Has a Problem

The conflict

Gets in the way of what they want, on three levels:

  • External — the physical problem
  • Internal — how it makes them feel
  • Philosophical — why it's plain wrong
Beat 03 — Meets a Guide

Your brand

Steps in with empathy — understanding their pain — and authority: competence and proof that you've solved this before.

Beat 04 — Gives a Plan

A clear path

Dispels fear with a simple process — often three steps — or an agreement that removes the customer's risk.

Beat 05 — Calls to Action

A clear invitation

A primary call to action ("Buy Now") plus a lower-stakes transitional one ("Download the Free PDF") for those not ready yet.

Beat 06 — Avoids Failure

What's at stake

The page names the negative consequences of doing nothing — the road the hero doesn't want to walk down.

Beat 07 — Ends in Success

The after

A vivid, specific picture of what the customer's life looks like once your product has done its job.

Implementing the framework

The grunt test

Within five seconds on your site, a visitor — even one distracted enough to be called a caveman — needs to answer three questions. If they can't, easylandx helps you rebuild until they can.

  1. 1

    What do you offer?

    Stated plainly, above the fold, in words a distracted person recognizes on first read.

  2. 2

    How will it make my life better?

    The benefit, not the feature list — tied to the success beat of the framework above.

  3. 3

    How do I buy it?

    One obvious next step, not three competing buttons and a maze of navigation.
