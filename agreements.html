The Dream of the Planet

Why we're domesticated

From childhood, rewards and punishments teach us who to be — building an Ideal Self alongside an inner Judge and Victim that never stop grading us against impossible standards.

Everything we perceive is a shared dream. Much of our suffering comes from agreements we never chose — built on fear and assumption. Personal freedom begins the moment we notice the dream and start re-writing the terms.

Be Impeccable With Your Word

Don't Take Anything Personally
