Four tools behind every EasyLandX page

Pick a tool to see how it shapes the pages we build for you.

Strip the page down to basic truths

We set aside “that’s how landing pages have always looked” and rebuild from what is true.

  • Who is the visitor, and what single action helps them?
  • Which sections earn their place for that action?
  • Which parts exist only out of habit? They go.
Ask: what is true here, and what is just habit?

Imagine the launch missed, then trace it back

Before publishing, we picture the page falling short of its goal and write down every reason that could explain it. Then we fix those reasons first.

  • Headline unclear in the first three seconds?
  • Button hidden below the fold?
  • Promise bigger than the proof?
Ask: if this missed, what would be the story?

AI writes the draft. A human signs it off.

Our AI-assisted drafts are treated like work from an eager assistant: useful, fast and always reviewed before they reach your visitors.

  • Facts and numbers checked against real sources
  • Tone matched to your brand and audience
  • Context added that only you can see
Ask: what would I need to confirm before I trust this?

Better questions before quick agreement

In our reviews, every default position gets a probing question first. The team agrees once the page has earned it.

  • What makes us believe this headline works?
  • What would change our mind?
  • Who sees this differently, and why?
