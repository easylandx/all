The big idea

Place the organizing in the page, so the mind can shine.

Our brains grew up in a world of simple, immediate choices. Today’s world offers petabytes of information and hundreds of micro-decisions every day. Daniel J. Levitin’s The Organized Mind shows a joyful path: move the organizing out of the head and into the environment. A landing page is an environment. easylandx builds it so visitors feel clear, energized, and ready to say yes.

Four attention networks, four design gifts

Executive Network

The focused, planning mind. Landing pages built with one clear goal per screen let it glide to a decision with energy to spare.

Default Mode Network

The daydreaming, connecting mind. Generous white space and gentle motion invite imagination and curiosity.

Attentional Switch

Moves focus between networks. One scroll path, one main button: visitors keep their energy for what matters.

Attentional Filter

Welcomes what is new and meaningful. A distinctive headline and a bold accent color earn attention in a single glance.

Five lessons, five easylandx strategies

1

Externalize memory

Writing ideas down lets the brain relax and refocus.

Page strategy: show the whole journey in view, with clear sections, labeled steps, and a visible next move.

2

Use spatial memory

A dedicated place for everything makes finding effortless.

Page strategy: consistent placement for headline, proof, and button, so visitors always know where to look.

3

Single-task focus

One thing at a time keeps the mind sharp and fast.

Page strategy: one offer, one action, one gorgeous call-to-action per landing page.

4

Simplify decisions

Every choice uses the same pool of executive energy.

Page strategy: a curated path with a confident default, so saying yes feels easy and good.

5

Protect rest and wandering

Downtime sparks original connections.

Page strategy: calm pacing, breathing room, and smooth animation that rewards each scroll.
