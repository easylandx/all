Every page is making a case

In Bargaining for Advantage, Wharton professor G. Richard Shell shows that the best negotiators don't overpower the other side — they out-prepare and out-listen them, gathering the information that lets both sides reach a confident yes.

EasyLandX treats every landing page the same way: as an ongoing exchange of information with your visitor. We anticipate their questions, answer their real concerns, and frame your offer around standards they already trust — so saying yes feels like the obvious, easy choice.

Six Foundations, Built Into Every Page

The same building blocks Shell uses to prepare any negotiation, applied to how EasyLandX plans your page.

01 · STYLE

Your brand's natural voice

We start by learning how your business naturally communicates, then shape the page's tone to match it closely.

02 · GOALS

Target outcome & baseline

We define the ambitious result you're aiming for, and the minimum result that still makes the page worthwhile.

03 · STANDARDS

Trust signals & norms

Testimonials, credentials, and familiar benchmarks show visitors your offer matches standards they already believe in.

04 · RELATIONSHIPS

Give before you ask

Helpful answers and clear value come first, building goodwill before the page ever asks a visitor for their details.

05 · INTERESTS

What visitors truly want

Copy is shaped around the real motivation behind a visit — not just the surface-level request.

06 · LEVERAGE

A page worth choosing

A clear, distinct offer that makes your business the obvious choice, not just one option among many.

The Deal-Closing Roadmap

Five stages that take a page from first draft to a confident yes.

01

Prepare the foundations

Style, goals, standards, relationships, interests, and leverage — all mapped before design begins.

02

03

Anchor & frame
