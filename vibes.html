The Big Idea

Vibing is a skill, and your landing page can practice it.

In Vibe, Adam Grant shows that chemistry is a learnable discipline: mutual resonance, psychological safety, and shared experiences. A landing page is a first meeting at scale. Design it with intention and visitors feel welcome right away.

1

Built, not found

Chemistry grows through intentional practice. Your page builds it step by step: headline, proof, invitation.

2

Solidarity over similarity

Visitors do not need to match you. They need to share your purpose. Lead with the outcome you both want.

3

Closeness is quick

High trust can form fast under the right conditions. Clear promises and real testimonials set those conditions in seconds.

4

Friction deepens bonds

Honest answers to tough questions (price, timing, fit) create deeper trust than flawless polish.
