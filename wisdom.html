Why most web services get it wrong

Two ways to lose a customer

Every landing page fails for one of two reasons — and neither has anything to do with your business.

The tyranny of templates

Rules instead of judgment

Drag‑and‑drop builders hand every business the same twelve layouts. The rules prevent disaster — but they also prevent excellence. Your bakery gets the same page structure as a law firm, because the system was never built to read your context, only to enforce a format.

The result: a page that looks fine and converts nobody.

The corruption of incentives

Metrics instead of outcomes

Agencies bill by the hour, upsell by the add‑on, and measure success by how fast a project closes — not by whether it actually brings you customers. The incentive quietly shifts from "does this work for you" to "does this hit our numbers."

The result: a page optimized for the invoice, not for your business.
