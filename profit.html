The Flawed Formula

Conversions shouldn't be a leftover.

Traditional site-building treats conversion as whatever's left after design, content and features are done. Because attention and budget always get spent on what's in front of you, that leftover is usually nothing.

How most sites get built
Traffic − Content = Conversions

Pages, sections and features are added until the budget runs out — and conversion, the actual goal, is whatever's left over. Usually that's close to zero.

The EasyLandX way
Traffic − Conversions = Content

We decide what makes a visitor act, build and fund that first, and let everything else — copy, imagery, extra pages — fill in what's left. It's a small flip that changes every design decision that follows.

Four Working Principles

How we keep a page honest.

01

Build smaller pages

A page with room for ten ideas will eventually hold ten ideas — and lose the one that mattered. We size every page to fit only what earns its place, so there's nowhere for the important thing to hide.

02

Serve sequentially

The call-to-action is designed before the hero copy, and the hero before the footer. Build in the other order and the last thing built — usually the thing that should convert — gets whatever attention is left.

03

Remove the temptation

No stray navigation, no competing offers, no third link out of the page. If it doesn't support the one action you want taken, it doesn't belong above the fold — or anywhere else.

04

Keep a rhythm

Pages aren't tweaked daily on a whim. We review performance on a fixed cadence, so changes come from real patterns in the numbers — not from chasing every small fluctuation.
