Phase One — Before

Turn strangers into people who raise their hand

Before anyone buys, they need to notice you, understand you, and believe you're speaking to them specifically. Your landing page is built to do exactly that.

01

Target Market

We don't design pages for "everybody." We build for the niche you want more of — so a tarot reader's page doesn't look, sound, or read like a sushi bar's. Specific beats generic, every time.

02

Message

No mission statements. Your headline is built around one clear promise: the problem your visitor has, and the solution you offer — said plainly, in the first five seconds.

03

Media

Instagram, WhatsApp, Google, word of mouth — wherever your prospects already are, your page is built to receive that traffic and convert it, with tracking so you know what's working.

Phase Two — During

Build trust before you ask for the sale

Nobody buys from a stranger. Your page is sequenced to earn trust first — so by the time you ask for the booking, the answer is already yes.

04

A booking form, a WhatsApp button, a simple contact field — every page ships with a way to capture a "maybe" before it disappears, so a visit becomes a real lead.

05

Nurture

Layout and copy do the educating: your story, your work, your proof — laid out in the order that builds confidence, not just the order that looks nice.

06

Sales Conversion

Testimonials, clear next steps, and friction removed from the path to booking — because a beautiful page that doesn't convert is just decoration.
