Framework Two

The 5 Levels of Market Sophistication

Every niche gets more crowded over time. EasyLandX evolves your page's core promise to match how much marketing your visitors have already seen.

LVL 1

The First Claim

A brand-new niche. State the promise plainly: "Get a landing page in 48 hours."

LVL 2

The Enlarged Claim

Competitors match the promise. Push it further: "A page that converts from day one, not month three."

LVL 3

The Mechanism

Visitors are numb to promises. Reveal the "how": our conversion-first layout system, tuned per industry.

LVL 4

The Advanced Mechanism

Others copy the mechanism. Upgrade it: real client data, live WhatsApp buttons, scroll-reveal psychology, built in.

LVL 5

Identity

The market is fatigued by mechanisms. EasyLandX becomes a statement: "This is a business that takes itself seriously."
