The playbook in three phases.

Each phase has to be earned before the next one begins.

1

Validate the pain

  • Discovery interviews. Talk to people and stay silent about your product. Listen for raw frustration, not polite praise. Use what you hear as your page copy.
  • Atomic ICP. One persona, one acute pain. Build one landing page per niche rather than one page for the whole market.
2

Build the solution

  • Minimum Viable Offering. Sell the result, not a feature list. One promise, one button.
  • Concierge or Wizard of Oz. Make a polished page, then deliver the service by hand. Confirm people will pay before you write complex code.
  • Minimum Delightful Experience. Give a visitor a “wow” in their first eight seconds.
3

Sell and distribute

  • The 5-5-5 cadence. Five cold outreaches a day, five discovery conversations a week, five follow-ups for every piece of actionable feedback.
  • Launch quietly. Swap the big public launch for personal onboarding of early users, one by one, the way a sherpa guides a climb.
  • Founder-led sales. You are the chief evangelist. Master the sale before you hand it off.
