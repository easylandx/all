The story

A young archer, a wise teacher, and a pile of wood

In Chop Wood Carry Water by Joshua Medcalf, John travels to Japan with a bold dream: to become a legendary Samurai archer. His mentor, Sensei Akira, welcomes him with daily tasks of chopping wood and carrying water.

Through those simple chores and Akira's parables, John discovers that mastery grows from joyful commitment to the everyday routine. Every log and every bucket is archery practice for the heart.
