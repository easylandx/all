01 — the cost of clutter

Every extra link is a decision your visitor has to make.

Years of scrolling feeds and clicking links trained the brain to scan, not read — eyes moving in a rough "F," grabbing headlines and bailing on dense paragraphs. A cluttered homepage forces a decision at every link: click this, skip that, is this relevant to me? Multiply that by twenty nav items and a wall of text, and most visitors leave before they ever see your offer.
