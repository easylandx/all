The first minute shapes the whole conversation.

A clear opening gives your audience a happy start: they know the topic, the purpose and the outcome. Everything that follows lands with energy. The same idea powers a landing page: a clear first screen invites visitors to say yes.

1

Light on bandwidth

Intention over information

Four lessons, ready to use.

🧭 Context before content

Frame the topic and outcome first. Details then arrive in a welcoming setting.

🎯 Intent sets the stage

Decision, update or advice? Naming your intent guides attention where it helps most.

✂️ Brevity through preparation

A few well-chosen sentences turn complex ideas into crisp, memorable statements.

🤝 Pause and confirm

A friendly pause lets everyone absorb the message and confirm they are ready to move forward.

The GPS Method

Within 15 to 30 seconds, guide your audience in three steps. Tap each letter to explore.

G

Goal

Name the project, objective or topic you are celebrating.

“We are launching the EasyLandX landing page next week.”

P

Problem

Name the issue, obstacle or reason for the conversation in one clear line. It opens the door to a great solution.

“Our headline has room to welcome more visitors with a sharper message.”

S

Solution

Share your recommendation and the direction you propose.

“Option A, which converted 20% higher in testing.”

The Framing Structure

Three components shape a confident opening. Then you pass the mic.

C

Context

The overarching topic in one or two sentences.

I

Intent

What you would love from the conversation: “I’d love a decision on…” or “I’m sharing an update on…”

K

Key message

The most important takeaway in a single, shining statement.

Pass the mic ✨

After framing, pause and hand the conversation over. Tap to watch it unfold.

I’m following up on the landing page launch. I’d love your approval on the headline.
Option A converted 20% higher, so I recommend it.
Are you ready to review the options?
Yes, let’s explore it together! 🎉
