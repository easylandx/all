The EasyLandX attentional framework

Five steps built on how the brain actually filters information, adapted from cognitive science to page building. Tap each one to open it.

Externalize and brain-dump
Get every idea out of your head and into one inbox.
Headlines, images, offers, half-formed thoughts — all of it goes into one capture point before it goes near the page. Try the working-memory demo below to feel exactly where the overflow point is.
2
Categorize and systematize
Give every element a dedicated home.
Hero, proof, offer, action — each captured idea gets sorted into a clear category the moment it leaves your head, so nothing has to be searched for or re-decided later.
3
Batch and block attention
Work one section at a time, in one sitting.
Switching between sections mid-thought costs more than it feels like. Try the switch-cost simulator below to see the difference between batching and bouncing around.
4
Protect your rest mode
Step away and let ideas reconnect on their own.
EasyLandX auto-saves every draft, so a walk away from the screen costs nothing. Mind-wandering is where scattered ideas quietly find their best order.
5
Automate the small decisions
Let defaults handle what doesn't need to be chosen.
Fonts, spacing, and layout come pre-decided by default, so your attention only spends itself on the words and choices that are genuinely yours to make.
