The six illusions, applied to your website

What the book gets right about attention, we build directly into your page.

Each illusion below is a real, tested pattern in how people perceive things — and a specific decision EasyLandX makes so your landing page works with human attention instead of hoping against it.

01 — Illusion of Attention

People only see what they're focused on.

A visitor scanning their phone in three seconds is running their own version of the pass-counting task. Anything outside their narrow focus — your phone number, your price, your booking link — effectively doesn't exist to them.

EasyLandX response: one clear focal path per screen, so the one action you want them to take sits exactly where their attention already is.

02 — Illusion of Memory

First impressions get rebuilt, not replayed.

Visitors don't store your site like a recording. They reconstruct a fuzzy impression later, from whatever repeated the most: your name, your color, your one clear promise.

EasyLandX response: a consistent visual anchor — name, palette, and headline — repeated enough times that it's what survives in memory.

03 — Illusion of Confidence

Trust is read from polish, not claims.

People equate a confident, composed presentation with real expertise — long before they've verified anything. A cluttered, DIY-looking page reads as uncertain, no matter how good the business behind it is.

EasyLandX response: clean typography, deliberate spacing, and a finished feel that signals competence at a glance.

04 — Illusion of Knowledge

Familiarity is mistaken for understanding.

A visitor who's used a hundred websites believes they already know what yours offers — the same way people believe they understand a zipper because they use one daily, right up until asked to explain it.

EasyLandX response: the offer is spelled out in plain words, not implied — what you do, what it costs, how to start.

05 — Illusion of Cause

People connect events that happen close together.

A slow-loading page next to a bad decision to leave gets rewritten in a visitor's mind as "this business is unreliable" — a causal story built from a coincidence of timing.

EasyLandX response: a fast, single-file build with no lag between the click and the page, so the only story a visitor tells themselves is a good one.

06 — Illusion of Potential

There's no shortcut that unlocks instant results.

Just as there's no trick that unlocks hidden brainpower overnight, there's no template that unlocks conversions by itself. What works is disciplined craft, applied to your real content.

EasyLandX response: a page built specifically around your business, your photos, and your customers — not a generic shortcut wearing your logo.
