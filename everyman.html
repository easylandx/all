Economics for a page, not a spreadsheet

A site isn't a math problem to optimize into submission — it's a small institution of trust between a business and its customers. Our process treats it that way, replacing one big rational leap with five human-sized steps.

Start from lived experience, not a template01

We ask what your customers actually worry about and want before we open a design file — not what a generic funnel says they should want.

Build the trust structure first02

Prices, guarantees, contact details and reviews go up front. Markets only work when the rules are visible — so does a page.

Design around real behavior03

Hesitation is usually a rational response to risk, not a flaw to trick past. We answer the hesitation instead of hiding it.

Check who actually benefits04

Every layout choice is tested against one question: does this help the small business compete, or just look impressive in a portfolio?

Keep you in the decisions05

You approve the story before it goes live. It's your business's page, not an algorithm's guess at one.
