A note on how visitors actually decide

Nobody reads your landing page with one mind.

Every visitor runs on two engines at once — a fast, emotional one and a slower, reasoning one. Psychologists David DeSteno and Piercarlo Valdesolo spent their book Out of Character mapping that tug-of-war. We build pages around it.

The dual-engine mind

Speak to both, or you'll lose half the room.

The book's core claim: moral and practical choices come from a constant trade-off between short-term impulse and long-term calculation. A landing page faces the same split-second split.

The emotional engine

Fast, automatic, first to arrive. It reacts to your hero image, your headline, your color, your tone — before a single feature is read.

The reasoning engine

Slower, deliberate, arrives seconds later. It checks price, proof, and whether the claim actually holds up.
