Nine ideas, one page

Each of your business's core needs — being found, being trusted, being fast, being safe — maps to a well-worn idea from computer science. Here's how they show up in your page.

01 the word-location trick

Instant clarity index

Your offer, hours, and contact details are placed exactly where a visitor's eye and a search engine both look first — so the one thing they came for is never buried.

02 the random-surfer trick

Trust that travels

Pages are structured around real links — your Instagram, your WhatsApp, your reviews — so the same signals that build authority across the web build authority for you.

03 the paint-mixing trick

Secure by design

Contact forms, Stripe checkouts, and booking links run over the same one-way encryption that protects banking — your customers' details stay private in transit.

04 the redundancy trick

Built to never break

Fonts, images, and layouts carry fallbacks, so your page renders cleanly on an old Android, a fussy Safari, or a shaky connection — no visitor sees a broken page.

05 pattern recognition

Reading your customers

Design choices are drawn from patterns that genuinely convert for your kind of business — a tarot reader's page and a bakery's page ask visitors for different things, in different ways.

06 the leave-it-out trick

Fast by design

Images are compressed exactly as far as the eye can't tell the difference, so your page opens in an instant on any phone, on any connection.

07 the write-ahead log trick

Nothing lost in transit

Every booking, message, or form submission is captured reliably, the way a database protects a transaction — so an interrupted connection never costs you a lead.

08 the lockbox trick

Verified as genuinely yours

Real contact details, real social links, a consistent voice — visitors can tell instantly that they've reached your business, not an imitation of it.

09 the limits of logic

Honest about what's worth building

Not every feature earns its place. Just as some problems are provably unsolvable, some page ideas simply aren't worth the added weight — so we build only what genuinely helps you convert.
