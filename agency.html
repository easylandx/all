Framework

Behavioral + Cognitive strategies, built into every page

Behavioral · Body & Environment

  • Control Stimuli: a calm layout with one highlighted action creates mental space.
  • Associate Selectively: uplifting testimonials and community voices spread positive energy.
  • Move: flowing scroll, active buttons and step-by-step journeys energize visitors.

Cognitive · Mind & Decisions

  • Learner Mindset: curiosity turns every update into insight.
  • Emotions & Beliefs: self-aware, warm messaging builds trust.
  • Check Intuition: gut ideas shine brighter when confirmed with facts.
  • Deliberate, Then Act: plan thoughtfully, then launch decisively.
