How to think about the page

Three habits that separate a great page from a good one

01

Think forward, design backward

Start from the exact action you want a visitor to take, then work backward to choose the headline, image, and first sentence that leads them there most directly.

02

Find your equilibrium

The strongest pages reach a natural resting point — where the offer, the proof, and the price all line up so confidently that visitors have every reason to say yes.

03

Build steady cooperation

A single visit is one round of a longer relationship. Reward return visits, honor first impressions, and let trust compound over repeated interactions with your brand.

Put it into practice

Three moves you can build into your next page today

Commitment & Credibility

Make your promises easy to believe

  • Publish clear guarantees. A visible, specific promise reads as far more credible than a vague reassurance.
  • Show, don't summarize. Real numbers, real names, and real screenshots carry more weight than general claims.
  • Keep pricing in the open. Transparent pricing invites confident decisions instead of hesitation.
Signaling & Screening

Learn who's arriving, and let them learn about you

  • Signal with proof. Case studies, certifications, and client logos are costly to fake — which is exactly why they build trust.
  • Screen with smart forms. A short, well-placed question can reveal exactly what a visitor is looking for.
  • Match the message to the mover. Use what you learn to tailor the next section a visitor sees.
Mechanism Design

Shape the rules, shape the result

  • Reorder for momentum. Sequence sections so each one builds naturally on the confidence created by the last.
  • Design the offer itself. A well-structured bundle or tier can guide visitors toward the choice that suits them best.
  • Test the rules, not just the words. Changing structure and sequence often moves outcomes further than changing copy alone.
