The Engineered Page Process

Every page runs the same seven-station line

Selling isn't improvised, and neither is a page that converts. Seven stations, in order, every time.

01

Rapport

The opening tone of the page — human, specific, and immediately familiar to the visitor's situation.

02

Qualify

A short quiz or filter section that gets the visitor naming their own problem, out loud.

03

Build value

Proof and education, positioning the offer as the natural answer to what they just named.

04

Create desire

A clear picture of what changes once the problem is gone — the outcome, not just the feature.

05

Overcome objections

An honest FAQ that treats hesitation as a signal to answer, not a threat to dodge.

06

Close

One clear call-to-action. If the five stations before it did their job, this is just the next step.

07

Follow-up

A confirmation page and message sequence that turns a single conversion into a lasting relationship.
