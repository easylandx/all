The 10 landing page distortions

The thoughts keeping good businesses off the internet

Every distortion below is a real belief we hear from clients before their first EasyLandX page goes live — reframed from the ten classic cognitive distortions.

01 · All-or-Nothing Design

"If it isn't perfect, it's worthless."

One imperfect section means the whole page shouldn't exist.

Rational response: A clear, honest page live today outperforms a flawless one still sitting in drafts.

02 · Overgeneralization

"Nobody clicked yesterday, so nobody ever will."

One quiet day becomes a permanent verdict on the business.

Rational response: One data point is weather, not climate. We look at trends across weeks, not hours.

03 · Mental Filter

"That one bounce ruined everything."

One drop-off gets replayed on loop, five inquiries get ignored.

Rational response: We look at the whole funnel, not the one visitor who left in three seconds.

04 · Disqualifying the Positive

"That sale doesn't count, they already knew me."

Every win gets explained away instead of counted.

Rational response: A sale is a sale. Warm leads are still leads your page helped close.

05 · Mind Reading & Fortune Telling

"They'll think my prices are too high."

Predicting rejection before a single visitor arrives.

Rational response: We let the page state your value clearly and let real visitors answer — not your imagination.

06 · Magnification & Minimization

"That typo is a disaster."

Small flaws get catastrophized, real wins get shrunk to "just luck."

Rational response: Fixable things get fixed in minutes. Real wins get logged and repeated.

07 · Emotional Reasoning

"I feel invisible online, so I must be invisible."

A feeling gets treated as a fact about the business.

Rational response: Feelings are data about you, not verdicts on your traffic. We check the actual numbers together.

08 · "Should" Statements

"I should have a site like my competitor's."

Comparison becomes a source of guilt instead of direction.

Rational response: Your page should do one job well: turn your visitors into customers. Nobody else's page has to.

09 · Labeling

"I'm just not techy."

A skipped step becomes a permanent identity.

Rational response: "I haven't launched yet" is a status, not a trait. EasyLandX handles the technical part entirely.

10 · Personalization

"Slow season is my fault."

Industry-wide dips get taken personally.

Rational response: Some slowdowns belong to the season, not to you. We build the page so it's ready when demand returns.
