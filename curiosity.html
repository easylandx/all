Core Messages

Curiosity is an ethic

Being interested in the world is a responsibility, not a hobby — it dissolves tribalism and trades fear for wonder.

Science is a dialogue

Not a finished rulebook — a living conversation of better questions, tested assumptions, and cheerful correction.

Wonder beats dread

Cosmic insignificance isn't bleak — it's the privilege of being a conscious observer who gets to notice things.

Everyone qualifies

A scientific mindset is open-mindedness plus skepticism — anyone can build it, no white coat required.

Essential Lessons

Embrace "I don't know"

Admitting ignorance isn't failure — it's the doorway. Certainty stops inquiry; comfortable uncertainty opens it.

Reconnect art & science

The humanities/science divide is artificial. Science expands imagination; art gives facts their emotional weight.

Furlong your awe

No collider required — moss on a wall or a splashing raindrop restores daily joy just as well.
