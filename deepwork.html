The core dichotomy

Every hour in your business is either Deep or Shallow.

Newport's framework splits work into two modes. Website upkeep almost always lands in the second one — which is precisely why it's the first thing worth handing off.

Deep Work

What only you can do

Concentrated, high-value effort that grows your business and can't be copied by a competitor.

  • Serving your clients or customers
  • Refining your product, menu, or craft
  • Building real relationships in your community
Shallow Work

What EasyLandX takes on

Logistical, replicable tasks that feel urgent but rarely move your business forward.

  • Coding and hosting a landing page
  • Chasing layout and formatting fixes
  • Keeping links, buttons, and copy up to date
The four rules, applied

Deep Work's discipline, built into your page.

Newport's four rules for reclaiming focus map directly onto how EasyLandX designs and delivers your landing page.

01 — Work Deeply

One page, one job

Every EasyLandX page is built around a single ritual: get your visitor from curiosity to a booked call or a completed order, with nothing in the way.

02 — Embrace Simplicity

Clarity over clutter

Newport trains attention by removing novelty. We design the same way — a page that says exactly what your business does, without a wall of animations competing for the eye.

03 — Choose Your Tools Deliberately

Only what earns its place

The "craftsman" test: keep a tool only if its benefit clearly outweighs its distraction. Your page is built the same way — every button and section earns its place, or it's cut.

04 — Drain the Shallows

We take the logistics

Newport puts a hard ceiling on shallow work. We put a hard ceiling on yours — EasyLandX owns the building, hosting, and upkeep of the page, permanently.
