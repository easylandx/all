Big idea 1

Visitors read pages through schemas.

A schema is an inner map of connected concepts. New information either slots into the map (assimilation) or reshapes it (accommodation). Great landing pages know which one they are asking for.

Build a visitor’s map

Add elements to a page and watch the map respond. Familiar elements slot in. Fresh ones grow new branches.

Big idea 2

Working memory holds about four chunks.

Unorganized detail fills the mental workspace fast. Chunking turns many pieces into a few meaningful units, leaving room for the decision you want visitors to make.

The attention budget

Remove extraneous detail

Every extra widget competes with the one that matters. Keep what builds understanding, and give the rest its own page.

Chunk with purpose

Three sections with clear names beat twelve loose facts. Group, label, and let each chunk carry one idea.

Big idea 3

Expertise hides what beginners need.

The curse of knowledge makes insiders write in shorthand. Your visitor is a newcomer to your world. Make the thinking visible.
