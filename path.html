Core lessons

Four lessons that make your page unmissable.

Pick a lesson to see how it becomes a practical landing page move.

Lesson one

Put yourself out there with joy.

Bold personal branding and distinctive self-marketing make you unmissable. Your landing page is the perfect stage for it.

  • A hero headline that says who you are in one memorable line
  • Colors and shapes only you would choose
  • One clear button that invites the next step

Lesson two

Bring your ideas to where people gather.

Take your work into public spaces, online and in person, and let your audience find you in their favorite places.

  • Share your page link in the communities your audience loves
  • Add a QR code to prints, stickers and pop-ups
  • Greet visitors with a welcome that matches where they arrived from

Lesson three

Pair a sharp niche with a flexible range.

A distinct focus or signature style draws people in, and an adaptable set of skills spreads your opportunities wide.

  • A focused headline for your niche
  • A services row that shows your range
  • Room to add new offers as you grow

Lesson four

Choose the best way to create each project.

Partner up to pool resources and ideas, or lead solo to keep full creative control. Your page can celebrate both.

  • A "Let's create together" button for future partners
  • A solo showcase for the projects you lead
  • A credits section that celebrates everyone involved

Key strategies

Four moves to grow momentum.

Each strategy turns into a feature you can add to your EasyLandX page this week.

Guerrilla and mobile tactics

From the bookGrab attention with pop-up exhibitions, roving offices and handcrafted pieces sent straight to partners you admire.

Release early, release often

From the bookShare in-progress work to build momentum, gather feedback and show your work ethic in real time.

On your pageLaunch a simple page this week and add something new every week.

Repeat, improve, repeat

On your pageReuse your colors, shapes and voice everywhere so people recognize you instantly.

Pitch directly

From the bookOffer fresh solutions to clients for opportunities they may have yet to notice, ahead of formal job postings.

On your pageBuild a dedicated landing page for a client you admire and send it as your proposal.
