The big idea

Landing pages thrive on strategic subtraction

Inspired by Unhinged Habits by Jonathan Goodman: the secret to having more is choosing what to release. A landing page gains power when it steps out of "the middle" and goes all-in on what matters most. Here is how that works for your page.

🎯 Beyond the middle

Pages with a bold, singular message feel unforgettable. Visitors know exactly where to look and what to do next.

✂️ Subtraction wins

Every extra menu, badge, and paragraph asks for attention. Releasing them gives your headline and button room to shine.

🌗 Seasonal rhythm

Launch with intense energy, then rest and refine. Seasons keep your pages fresh and your energy high.

Obsession + Competence

🔥 World-class focus

Your headline, your offer, your call-to-action. These earn your full creative energy.

Key lessons

Intensity creates leaps

Intermittent intensity

Focused bursts of launch energy create bigger leaps than endless moderate effort.

Gain over perceived loss

A focused page celebrates what it offers. Visitors see the gain, so guilt over what is left out fades.

Presence over autopilot

Default layouts invite autopilot. Intentional pages invite attention.
