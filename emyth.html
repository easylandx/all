Fig. 01 — The problem

The landing page myth

Most small-business landing pages aren't built by someone who understands conversion. They're built by whoever's willing to stay up late and figure it out — the same person already running the business.

What actually happens

An electrician who's brilliant with wiring decides to also be brilliant at web copy, layout, and split-testing — because it's their business and no one else is going to do it. They know the trade. They don't know the page. So the page becomes whatever's fastest to ship, not what's built to convert.

What EasyLandX does instead

We separate the craft of your business from the craft of your landing page. You stay the expert in what you do. EasyLandX becomes the expert in the page that sells it — a system built once, correctly, and left to run.
