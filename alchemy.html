The EasyLandX formulary

Four transmutations, borrowed from Rory Sutherland

Alchemy argues that the biggest wins come from reframing, not rebuilding. We apply the same four moves to every page we build.

Feature list ↦ Real reason

Write for the real reason, not the logical excuse

Ask customers why they buy and they'll hand you a rational cover story. We don't write your headline around specs — we write it around the status, relief, or ambition actually driving the click, then let the features back it up underneath.

Wrong baseline ↦ Right comparison

Reframe what your price is compared against

A landing page priced against a free template looks expensive. Priced against a missed sale, a churned lead, or an agency invoice, it looks like the cheapest decision you'll make all quarter. We build the page — and the framing — around the comparison that actually favors you.

Slow perception ↦ Fixed experience

Fix the feeling of friction, not just the infrastructure

You don't always need a rebuild — you need the waiting removed. A visible launch timeline, instant previews, no back-and-forth with a developer: we fix the experience of getting a page live, which is usually the actual pain point.

Cheap look ↦ Visible intent

Let quality signal that you're serious

People subconsciously read a well-made page as proof a business intends to stick around. A considered layout and clean copy do real sales work before anyone reads a word — it's the cheapest trust signal you can buy.
