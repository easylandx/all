The reset sequence

A simple order for meeting any habit with curiosity instead of judgment — track it, understand it, notice its ripple, then give the brain room to recalibrate.

D

Data

Log what, how much, and when the habit shows up — plain observation, nothing more.

O

Objectives

Name the emotional need the habit is quietly meeting — comfort, connection, escape, ease.

P

Patterns

Notice how energy, focus, and closeness with others move alongside the habit over time.

A

Abstinence — the 30-day reset

Set the habit aside for four full weeks, giving the reward pathways room to recalibrate.

M — Mindfulness

Sit with the quiet moments, fully present.

I — Insight

Notice mood and clarity settling into place.
