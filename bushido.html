Bushido, applied to the web

In 1899, Inazo Nitobe wrote Bushido: The Soul of Japan to explain the unwritten code that shaped the samurai — a fusion of Buddhist composure, Shinto loyalty, and Confucian duty, built entirely around honor, courtesy, and sincere action.

EasyLandX borrows that same discipline for the craft of building landing pages. Every project we ship for a small business follows the same seven virtues that guided the warrior's path — turned into a practical standard for design, copy, and craftsmanship.

The Seven Virtues, Built Into Every Page

Tap any virtue to see how it shapes the way EasyLandX designs, writes, and ships your landing page.

義
GI · Rectitude One clear, honest purpose Every page opens with a single, true promise

Rectitude is the backbone of character — deciding on the right course and holding to it. On an EasyLandX page, that means the headline says exactly what the business offers, the value is stated plainly, and every visitor knows precisely what they're being invited to do.

Applied as: a single clear promise above the fold
勇
YU · Courage Decisive design, confident calls to action Bold layouts that move visitors forward

Courage is righteous action taken without hesitation. We design pages that commit fully to one strong idea and one confident call to action, so visitors always know exactly what step to take next.

Applied as: one hero, one clear action
仁
JIN · Benevolence Every layout built around the visitor Compassion for how people actually browse

Benevolence shines brightest when it protects the vulnerable. Applied to design, that means fast load times, generous spacing, and legible type — a page that meets a first-time visitor with genuine care rather than friction.

Applied as: fast, gentle, easy-to-read pages
礼
REI · Respect & Courtesy Polished craft in every detail Courtesy expressed through careful design

Courtesy is love in action, not empty etiquette. We treat alignment, spacing, and typography as an expression of respect for the visitor — every pixel considered, nothing left careless.

Applied as: refined typography and spacing
誠
MAKOTO · Sincerity Words that match reality Copy as good as a spoken promise

To a samurai, a spoken intention carried the full weight of a written oath. Every word on an EasyLandX page is held to that same standard — the copy says only what the business can truly deliver, plainly and transparently.

Applied as: honest copy, transparent pricing
名誉
MEIYO · Honor A brand's true character, on display Design that earns trust at first glance

Personal dignity was the samurai's measure of worth. For a small business, a landing page carries that same weight — it's often the very first impression. We build pages that represent the business's true character with pride and consistency.

Applied as: consistent, trust-building branding
忠
CHUGI · Loyalty & Duty A lasting partnership, not a one-time build Faithful support long after launch

Loyalty to one's calling was the samurai's highest duty. EasyLandX stays committed to every client's growth well beyond delivery day — refining, supporting, and standing behind each page we build.

Applied as: ongoing care for every client
