Two brain networks. One beautiful page.

Daniel J. Levitin's The Organized Mind shows that attention flows between a Central Executive Network for focused work and a Default Mode Network for rest and creative insight. EasyLandX pages welcome both.

Lessons turned into design

Six strategies from The Organized Mind, built into every page

Each lesson from Levitin's research becomes a practical EasyLandX design choice, so your visitors feel organized, informed and ready to act.

Externalize everything

Every detail has a home

Brains shine at processing, so great pages hold the details for them: prices, hours, steps and contact info sit in clear, visible places.

On your page: a tidy information map visitors can trust.
The categorization principle

Logical categories

When the layout mirrors how people naturally sort ideas, finding answers feels effortless and saves precious mental energy.

On your page: labeled sections for services, proof, pricing and contact.
Batching & rule creation

Similar ideas, grouped together

Grouping related information into one block lets visitors absorb it in a single, satisfying pass.

On your page: three benefits in one row, testimonials in one strip.
Pre-mortem planning

Pre-flight confidence

Anticipating every visitor question ahead of time turns a good page into a dependable one, like a trusted checklist.

On your page: FAQs, guarantees and clear next steps answered in advance.
Satisficing

Good enough becomes great

Simple, well-framed options let visitors decide confidently and quickly, saving their best thinking for what matters most.

On your page: three friendly packages with one recommended pick.
Protect attention & rest

Space to breathe

Generous spacing and a calm rhythm invite the daydreaming mind to connect ideas and imagine working with you.

On your page: airy layouts, soft contrast and a relaxed reading pace.
