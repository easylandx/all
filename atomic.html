Essential lessons

What the compounding curve actually looks like.

The plateau of latent potential

A new page rarely takes off on day one. Traffic builds, trust builds, search ranking builds — quietly — before the results become visible all at once. Keep shipping through the flat part.

Environment is the invisible hand

A visitor doesn't need more motivation — they need fewer obstacles. Put the signup field where the eye already lands, and remove every extra click between interest and action.

The two-minute rule

If a visitor can't understand your offer in two minutes, the page is doing too much. EasyLandX keeps the first fold readable, and the first action completable, in well under that.
