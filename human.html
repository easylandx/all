The four rules

Four rules, applied to every page

Tap a rule to see how it shapes the way EasyLandX pages get made.

Always invite AI to the table

Test it on headlines, layouts, objections, FAQs and microcopy. You learn the frontier only by trying, so every page section gets an AI first draft to react to.

Be the human in the loop

AI can confidently invent a testimonial or a statistic. A human checks every claim, price, promise and contact detail before a visitor sees it.

Tell it who it is

Give the AI a persona, context and goal. "Act as a direct-response copywriter for a plumber in a small coastal town" beats "write a landing page" every time.

This is the worst AI you will ever use

Tools improve fast. A page process built on adaptable habits, not fixed tricks, keeps getting better as the models do.
