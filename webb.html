The method

Three capabilities. One landing page.

Caroline Webb's Leadership Intelligence sorts 21 everyday management challenges into Wisdom, Inspiration and Resilience. Each one maps directly onto how we build pages for you.

1. Wisdom

Better decisions under pressure. Working memory is tiny, so stressed minds grab shortcuts and biases.

  • Beat confirmation bias before you publish
  • Protect mental bandwidth: one page, one choice
  • Use pre-mortems to find the weak spots

2. Inspiration

People act when the brain reads reward, not threat. Clarity, autonomy, trust and safety beat charisma.

  • Lead with common ground, not positions
  • Remove anything that feels like judgment or pressure
  • Frame every step as future growth

3. Resilience

Uncertainty triggers the amygdala. Steady pages and steady owners keep deciding well when things wobble.

  • Label the worry, then answer it on the page
  • Focus on what you control and influence
  • Reframe every setback as data

Framework

Feedback that drives action

Every EasyLandX revision round uses Webb's three parts, so changes feel like co-creation, not criticism.

01

Observe

Name a specific behavior. "Visitors scroll past the booking form without clicking."

02

Explain

State the impact. "That costs you enquiries from people who were already interested."

03

Ask

Invite solutions. "What would make the next step feel obvious to them?"

Common ground

Start from the outcome you both want

Visitors do not need convincing about your position. They need to see their own goal reflected back. Every page we build opens with shared intent: "What outcome do we both agree we want?" Then it shows the shortest, safest route there.

Use cognitive reframing when numbers dip: ask "What is one small opportunity or lesson hidden in this?" and let the next version carry the answer.
