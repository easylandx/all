The template mill

Live fast, forgotten faster

A page goes up in an afternoon, built off the same block anyone could buy. It looks like every other site running the same theme, so it stops working the moment it's noticed.

The DIY builder

Fighting the drag-and-drop

Nights spent forcing a page builder to behave. It breaks on a phone, the layout never quite settles, and it's never really finished — just abandoned mid-edit.

The one-and-done agency

Built once, touched never again

A decent page, shipped and handed off. No one checks back when the offer changes or the business moves on. The relationship ends at invoice.
