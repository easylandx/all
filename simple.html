Four Keys To The Real Numbers

The framework we build every page against.

Key 01

The Real Cost of "Free"

Building your own page yourself feels free because no invoice ever arrives. But your time has a market rate, and every week the page underperforms has a cost too — you're just not the one writing it down.

Rule: price your own time and your lost conversions at market rate, or you don't actually know your page's cost.
Key 02

The Traffic Black Hole

Between a trickle of visitors and real scale sits a dangerous stretch: enough traffic to expose a weak page, not enough to make the losses obvious. Businesses pour money into ads here and never notice the page is the leak.

Key 03

Conversion Efficiency Ratio

The Metric That Matters
Revenue From The Page ÷ What You Spent Building It

Forget vanity numbers like page views or time-on-site. This ratio tells you exactly how many dollars come back for every dollar spent on the page — and whether it's time to invest more, hold steady, or rebuild.

Key 04

The Order Results Get Used

Once a page is actually converting, the payoff doesn't get spent randomly — it gets used in a strict order:

Layer 1

Fix what's broken

Dead links, slow load times, unclear offers — resolved immediately, before anything else.

Layer 2

Retire the old page

Fully replace outdated templates and copy — no half-migrated, half-old pages left live.

Layer 3

Build a testing buffer

Bank a reserve of tested headline, CTA and layout variants before spending more on traffic.

Layer 4

Scale spend

Only once Layers 1–3 are handled does it make sense to put real budget behind driving more visitors.
