Core concept

Positioning is context setting.

A museum's wall label tells you whether an object is art or an artifact. Your market category does the same for your product: it decides how buyers judge price, features and rivals. Pick a frame and watch the verdict change.

Poor positioning means long sales cycles, low conversion and constant price complaints. Good positioning makes the advantage obvious to the ideal buyer.
