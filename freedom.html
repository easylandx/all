Action steps

Five moves to a lighter calendar

Design a seasonal schedule

Concentrate your client work into a few intense seasons. Let your landing page keep collecting interest during the breaks, with an auto-reply and a clear “back in September” note.

Build an unscalable lifestyle business

Resist scaling for scaling’s sake. One focused page, one offer, high margins, low overhead, few hours. Small on purpose.

Use a “no-budget” budget

Automate cash flows instead of tracking every coffee. Keep your page’s subscription and hosting costs simple and predictable too.

Keep heavy cash reserves

A deep cushion makes time away feel calm. Try the cash slider above and watch your runway grow.

Plan for zero income in year one

Assume your new venture earns nothing at first. That runway prevents scarcity-driven choices, and lets you test headlines and offers patiently.
