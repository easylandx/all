    Four habits of confident players

    ♜ Safety first

    Ask "Is it safe?" before every move. For pages: preview on a phone, a laptop and a slow connection, then press launch with a smile.

    ♛ Golden Pause

    When a move looks instantly brilliant, double-check every line. When a headline feels perfect, read it aloud once more and invite a friend's fresh eyes.

    ♞ Time with purpose

    Match effort to importance. Invest the most minutes in your hero section and main button, and finish with a fully used, well-spent clock.

    ♚ Clean when ahead

    Ahead in the game? Simplify to a clear win. Ahead in attention? Keep one message, one button, one beautiful path.
