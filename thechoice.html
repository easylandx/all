Six shortcuts, rebuilt for landing pages

Every EasyLandX block maps to a documented bias in consumer decision-making. Here's what each one looks like on a real page.

Pratfall Effect

Let one honest flaw sell the rest

A pricing page that admits "no free trial, but we answer support in under an hour" reads as more trustworthy than one claiming to be perfect. EasyLandX's pricing block has a built-in slot for one honest caveat.

Trust block
Social Proof

Show the crowd, not the shame

"12,400 pages published this month" converts. "Only 3% of sites convert well" backfires — it normalizes failure. EasyLandX's proof widget only ever counts up, never down.

Proof widget
Framing Effect

Move the anchor, not the price

$29/month feels steep alone, and cheap next to a $99 tier, or reframed as "under $1 a day." EasyLandX's pricing block auto-generates the daily-cost and tier-comparison framing for you.

Pricing block
Habit

Target the moment, not the mood

People rarely switch tools out of the blue — they switch after a launch, a rebrand, a new role. EasyLandX pages are built to be shipped fast, so you can publish the moment a "life shock" opens the door.

Fast publish
Peak-End Rule

Design the last five seconds

Visitors forget most of a page but remember its best moment and its very last one. EasyLandX's confirmation screens are built as a designed "peak," not a default thank-you page.

Confirmation screen
Price Relativity

Choose your own comparison

A tool priced against "a full-time designer" looks cheap; priced against "a free template" it looks expensive. EasyLandX lets you set the reference point your price is judged against.

Ship checklist

Two habits before you hit publish

i.

Cut before you add

Every extra field, sentence or menu item is a small tax on attention. Before publishing, remove one thing from your EasyLandX page — the one that only makes sense to you.

ii.

Time your biggest ask

People buy more freely when relaxed than when rushed. Schedule your highest-stakes campaigns — new pricing, a big launch — for Friday evenings or weekend mornings using EasyLandX's built-in scheduler.
