02 — Principles, Not Posters

Four rules we actually use on every build.

Borrowed from the leadership principles that run a global operation — applied to something much smaller: your page, your customers, your Tuesday afternoon.

Customer Obsession

Start with the thumb, not the logo.

We open every project on a phone screen, not a desktop mockup, because that's where your customer actually finds you.

Ownership

It's still our page after you pay us.

We check load times and broken links weeks after launch — a page that stops working is still our problem.

Bias for Action

Most decisions are reversible. So we ship.

A button color or a headline can change in five minutes. We'd rather have it live and adjust than perfect it in a folder.

Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit

We'll say if it won't convert.

If a request will quietly hurt your bookings, we say so once, clearly — then build exactly what you decide.

03 — No Slides, Just Pages

We banned the slide deck. We write the brief instead.

Every project starts with one dense page describing what we're building and why — not a slideshow that hides weak logic behind a nice transition. Before any client call, everyone reads it in silence first.

05 — Inputs Over Outputs

We chase the numbers we can actually control.

Bookings and calls are outcomes — they follow from decisions made earlier. So we obsess over the inputs and trust the outputs to arrive.

Inputs we control

Page loads in under 2 seconds

Outputs that follow

Higher trust before first contact
Referrals from a page worth sharing
06 — The Bar Raiser

Nothing launches until it clears the bar.

One person on every project has veto power over launch — not to slow things down, but to make sure every page we ship is better than the last one, not just fast to finish.

✓

Mobile-first pass: every section checked on a real phone, not just resized in a browser.

✓

Contact paths work: WhatsApp, Instagram, and phone links all tested by hand before launch.

✓

No dead weight: no external images or scripts that can silently break the page later.

✓

Sound and motion respect the reader: nothing autoplays, everything can be muted in one tap.
