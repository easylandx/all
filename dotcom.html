03 — How every page gets built

Five questions, answered in order, before a single line of copy is written.

01

Who is this actually for?

Not "everyone in the neighborhood" — the specific person most likely to say yes, and what they're worried about before they book.

02

What stops the scroll?

The opening line or image has to earn the next five seconds. If it doesn't, nothing below it gets read.

03

What's the story?

Not a list of features — a short, honest account of the problem this business solves and why it's worth trusting with it.

04

What's the one offer?

One clear thing to do next — message on WhatsApp, book a slot, place an order — never three competing buttons.

05

What happens to the ones who don't say yes yet?

Not every visitor converts on the first visit. The page is built so a business can follow up — by WhatsApp, Instagram, or email — instead of losing them for good.

04 — Three kinds of pages we build

The right structure depends on what you're selling.

SIMPLE OFFER

The quick-capture page

Best for a single product, a booking, or a low-cost service. One scroll, one clear ask, built to load fast on a phone.

Fits: shops, groomers, cafés, artisans
CONSIDERED OFFER

The full-story page

Best when the customer needs convincing first. Longer scroll, real testimonials, and a clear breakdown of what they get.

Fits: coaches, studios, courses, retreats
HIGH-TOUCH OFFER

The application page

Best for premium, limited-availability work. The page qualifies the visitor first, so you only hear from serious leads.

Fits: consultants, high-end services
