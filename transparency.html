The 4.2 Rule

A flawless claim reads as a fake one

Products rated a perfect 5.0 convert worse than ones rated 4.2–4.5 — buyers assume perfect scores are filtered. The same pattern holds for landing page claims: "the best ever" reads as noise, while a page that names one honest limitation reads as real.

Flaunting Your Flaws

Cards Face Up

Trade, don't just discount

A discount with no reason attached reads as desperation. A discount tied to a clear trade reads as fair — and belongs right on the pricing section of your page.
